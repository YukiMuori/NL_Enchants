## Effect

25%/35% chance (by level) to negate each point of durability damage (official unbreaking mechanic with custom chance).

## VFX

None by design — silent sustainability.

## Balance

Stronger than vanilla Unbreaking I at II (~33% equivalent), weaker than Unbreaking II+; treasure-free pool weighting keeps it accessible.
