## Effect

On arrow impact: a wind burst (mace-style knock-up) plus a collectible Wind Charge item — the renewable part of the source description.

## VFX

Gust particles + breeze charge sound at the impact point.

## Notes

- ADAPTATION: the source spawns a wind charge when the arrow hits a BLOCK; ME has no projectile-block-impact trigger (verified trigger list) — fires on entity impact instead. windburst is a MythicEnchants mechanic → direct lines only (R8).
